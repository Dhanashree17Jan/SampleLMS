Supporting coverage: Authentication/users (`auth.md`), master/location/data requirements (`master.md`, `trainee.md`), stable template versions (`template.md`), role/record/antiforgery/transport security (`sec.md`), responsive/accessibility/browser behavior (`ui.md`), integrity/atomicity/time zone/performance/backup/retention (`ops.md`). The four-day Pre form editor update is covered by TC-PLAN-015 through TC-PLAN-017.

Proposed defaults are tested as written in the Draft PRD: consecutive calendar days, minimum two days, one project per trainee, same-school eligibility, non-overlapping batches, identified feedback, online submissions, and missing feedback not blocking completion. Product decisions about weekends, one-day plans, anonymity, cross-school batches, multiple projects, late joining/withdrawal, partial attendance, user volumes/retention/hosting require approval before changing these expectations.

Out of first-release scope: video delivery, payments, certificates, payroll, biometric attendance, disconnected operation and separate pre/post assessment. No acceptance pass is implied for these exclusions.
